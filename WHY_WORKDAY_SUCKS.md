# Why Workday Application System is Awful for Job Seekers

## Executive Summary

Workday, one of the most widely used Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), is notorious among job seekers for creating one of the most frustrating application experiences in modern recruiting. Research shows that Workday's cumbersome process leads to **70% fewer applications** compared to streamlined systems, with a staggering **92% drop-off rate** for applicants who start but don't complete applications.

---

## Key Problems with Workday

### 1. **Repetitive Data Entry**

Despite uploading a resume, candidates are forced to manually re-enter their entire work history, education, and other details into separate fields.

> "The typical Workday application process feels like filling out the same information 100+ times, and despite candidates uploading their resumes, they're often required to manually input their work history, education, and other details into separate fields."

**Impact:** What should be a 5-minute application turns into a 30-minute ordeal.

**Sources:**
- [Why Candidates Hate Workday (And How to Fix It)](https://simplify.jobs/blog/why-candidates-hate-workday/)
- [Workday The Worst Application Experience](https://medium.com/@dyanbermeo_40758/workday-the-worst-application-experience-ba851ff6c59c)

### 2. **Terrible Resume Parsing**

Workday's resume parsing functionality is notoriously inaccurate, often getting "99 percent of everything wrong."

> "When applicants upload their resumes to a company site using Workday, it gets 99 percent of everything wrong. The parsing is awful."

This forces candidates to manually correct all the incorrectly parsed data, defeating the entire purpose of resume upload functionality.

**Sources:**
- [Jared Spool on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jmspool_ive-heard-rumblings-that-the-workday-application-activity-7143789755279843328-B64B)

---

### 3. **Multiple Account Creation Required**

One of the most criticized aspects is the requirement to create a separate Workday account for **every single company** using the platform.

> "One of the main pain points is the requirement to create a new account for every company using Workday - imagine a job seeker applying to ten different companies, that's TEN separate Workday accounts to create and manage."

Applying to 100 companies? You need 100 separate Workday accounts.

**Sources:**
- [Why Candidates Hate Workday (And How to Fix It)](https://simplify.jobs/blog/why-candidates-hate-workday/)
- [Fishbowl Discussion](https://www.fishbowlapp.com/post/so-sick-of-workday-when-applying-for-jobs-if-the-link-takes-me-to-workday-i-either-groan-and-commence-to-creating-yet-another-profile)

---

### 4. **Massive Application Drop-Off Rates**

The complexity and length of Workday applications cause severe abandonment:

- **92% drop-off rate** - people who click "Apply" but don't complete the application
- **60%** of job seekers quit mid-application due to length and complexity
- **70% fewer applications** compared to companies with streamlined processes

> "Appcast found the drop off rate to be 92% for people who click 'Apply,' but do not complete an application - companies are losing 92% of interested candidates before even having a chance to evaluate their qualifications."

**Sources:**
- [Why Candidates Hate Workday (And How to Fix It)](https://simplify.jobs/blog/why-candidates-hate-workday/)
- [Workday Job Applications Made Simple](https://www.jobwizard.ai/post/workday-job-applications-made-simple)

### 5. **Applicant Hostility & Avoidance**

Many job seekers actively avoid companies using Workday:

> "Many job seekers won't apply for a company that uses Workday - 'It's that bad.' When applicants see a job post has led to Workday they abandon it and apply someplace else, calling it '2025 not 2005.'"

The system has become so despised that seeing the Workday interface is enough to make candidates abandon applications entirely.

### 6. **Poor Mobile Experience**

Despite **2/3 of job searches happening on mobile devices**, Workday's mobile experience falls short, making it even more difficult for candidates applying on-the-go.

**Sources:**
- [Workday Job Applications Made Simple](https://www.jobwizard.ai/post/workday-job-applications-made-simple)

---

### 7. **Design Philosophy: Applicants Aren't the Customer**

The fundamental issue is that Workday sells to HR departments, not applicants:

> "The fundamental problem is that applicants are not the target market - Workday sells to the HR department so their user experience is the only one that matters."

This misalignment means the system is optimized for corporate workflows, not for the people actually applying for jobs.

**Sources:**
- [Ask HN: Why do companies use terrible job application portals like Workday?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=34408992)

---

### 8. **Incomplete Product & Technical Limitations**

- **74%** of Workday ATS users augment it with third-party tools to compensate for missing features
- According to a Workday analyst: "the applicant tracking side is a work in progress"

### 9. **Legal & Ethical Concerns**

A 2023 lawsuit (Julian Mobley case) argues that if ATS systems score and rank candidates in ways that disproportionately disadvantage certain groups, it constitutes a civil rights issue.

## The Business Impact

While Workday frustrates applicants, it also hurts companies:

- **70% reduction in application volume** means companies miss out on top talent
- **92% abandonment rate** means wasted recruiting marketing spend
- **Negative brand perception** among job seekers

Companies using Workday are inadvertently creating barriers that prevent qualified candidates from applying, while simultaneously damaging their employer brand.

---

## Why Companies Still Use It

Despite all these issues, companies continue using Workday because:

1. **Enterprise Integration** - It integrates with other Workday HR/Finance modules
2. **HR Department Focus** - It solves problems for HR teams, not applicants
3. **Switching Costs** - Large enterprises face significant costs to migrate away
4. **Lack of Awareness** - HR departments may not realize the extent of applicant frustration

---

## Conclusion

Workday's Applicant Tracking System represents a fundamental misalignment between corporate needs and candidate experience. While it may serve HR departments well, it creates significant friction for job seekers, leading to massive application abandonment, reduced talent pools, and widespread frustration.

**The data is clear:** Workday is legitimately awful for job applicants, and the numbers prove it.
